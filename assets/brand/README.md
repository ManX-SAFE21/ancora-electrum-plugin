# Àncòra — brand files

The logo of the plugin (#199, 2026-10-28): the "à" with two teal accents,
and the word "àncòra" with the slogan below it. Made by the owner; the
files here come from the owner's traced SVGs, with the colours and the
shapes unchanged: only the background was removed (the holes of the letters
became real holes) and the drawing was cut out with a small margin. Use the
files as they are: do not change the colours, the shapes or the accents.

- **Name:** Àncòra, the same in every language (in the logo: àncòra,
  lower case). In code and file names: `ancora`.
- **Slogan:** *Àncora per te, ancòra per loro.* (Italian in every
  language: the play on words only works in Italian; in the logo it is
  written in lower case).
- **Colours of the logo:** ink `#0D0E12` and teal `#1DAA9B` (the word);
  ink `#0F1113` and teal `#29AE9F` (the "à" of the icon).

| Files | What |
|---|---|
| `svg/ancora-mark.svg` | the "à" alone, transparent background |
| `svg/ancora-icon.svg` | the plugin's icon: the "à" at 90% of a white square with rounded corners (owner, 2026-10-28: it stands out on a dark theme too), used for `bal/icons/ancora-icon-*.png` |
| `svg/ancora-lockup.svg` | "àncòra" + slogan, for light backgrounds (the PDF report: `bal/icons/ancora-lockup.png` = `png/ancora-lockup-1200.png`) |
| `svg/ancora-lockup-dark.svg` | the same for dark backgrounds: the word in white `#FEFEFD` |
| `png/` | the same, as images (icon 16–1024 px, lockup 1200 and 2400 px wide) |

The printed logo is always on a light background (white paper).
